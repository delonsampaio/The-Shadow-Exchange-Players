## The game in one breath
You play a small monster-hunting firm. Whenever you attempt something risky or uncertain, you **roll a pool of d6**. Most rolls produce *both* what you wanted *and* a cost — that's the point.

## The only dice rule you need
Roll as many six-sided dice as your **rating** in that action. **Look at the highest one.**

| Highest die | What happens |
|---|---|
| **6** | You do it, clean. |
| **4–5** | You do it, **but** there's a cost. *(The most common result.)* |
| **1–3** | It goes wrong. Something bad happens. |
| Two 6s | A critical — extra good. |

## Two things the GM will say before every roll
- **Position** — how bad it is *if it goes wrong*: **Controlled** (safe-ish) · **Risky** (default) · **Desperate** (cornered).
- **Effect** — how much you get *if it goes right*: **Limited** · **Standard** · **Great**.

You can negotiate these. *"Can I go slower to be safer?"* is a great question.

## Stress — your reserve of willpower
You spend **Stress** to push harder, help a friend, or **resist** a consequence. At 9 you take a **Trauma** — permanent, and it makes your character *more* interesting, not worse. You clear Stress in Downtime by indulging your **Vice**.

## Resist it — it always works
When something bad happens, you can say *"I resist."* You roll, and the **higher the die, the cheaper it is** (a 6 is free). Think of it as a reaction you declare *after* seeing the consequence land.

## Flashbacks — plan after the fact
Realize mid-job that you needed a key, a contact, or a disguise? You can say *"I flash back to having done that,"* for **0–2 Stress**. It's the opposite of D&D planning, and it's fun.

## The 12 actions (you say what you do — the GM names the action)

**INSIGHT (thinking):** Hunt (track/shoot a target) · Study (research; read a person or clue) · Survey (spot trouble; case a room) · Tinker (locks, gadgets, field surgery)

**PROWESS (body):** Finesse (sleight of hand, driving, precise fighting) · Prowl (sneak, climb, ambush) · Skirmish (brawl up close) · Wreck (smash, breach, chaos)

**RESOLVE (will):** Attune (sense the supernatural) · Command (intimidate, give orders) · Consort (friends & contacts) · Sway (lie, charm, argue)

*(0 dots = roll two dice and take the **lower**.)* **Bonus:** whenever you roll a **Desperate** action, you mark **1 XP** right away — daring is rewarded.
