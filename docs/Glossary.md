# Glossary

*Plain-English meanings. If a word isn't here, ask — nobody will mind.*

## The game
- **Action roll** — roll as many six-sided dice as your rating in that skill; look at the **highest**. 6 = clean win, 4–5 = win with a cost, 1–3 = it goes wrong.
- **Position** — how bad it is if the roll goes wrong: **Controlled**, **Risky**, **Desperate**.
- **Effect** — how much you accomplish if it goes right: **Limited**, **Standard**, **Great**.
- **Stress** — your reserve of willpower (max 9). Spend it to push harder, help a friend, or resist.
- **Trauma** — what happens when you hit 9 Stress. Permanent, and it makes your character more interesting.
- **Resistance** — you can resist a consequence. Roll, and the higher the die the cheaper it is. A 6 is free.
- **Flashback** — say you prepared something earlier. Costs 0–2 Stress.
- **Devil's Bargain** — the GM offers +1 die in exchange for a complication.
- **Engagement roll** — the first roll of a job; it decides how the job starts.
- **Score** — a job. **Downtime** — what you do between jobs. **Entanglement** — a problem that finds you after a job.
- **Playbook** — your character type. **Vice** — the unhealthy thing you do to clear Stress.
- **Crew XP / Rep / Tier** — how the crew grows: *Rep* is reputation, *Tier* is how big and powerful the crew is (you start at 0).
- **Heat** — how much attention the crew has drawn. **Coin** — money.

- **Attribute (Insight / Prowess / Resolve)** — three groups of four skills. Your attribute rating is **how many skills in that group have at least 1 dot** (0–4). You roll it to **resist**, and your *lowest* one for your **Vice**.
- **Zero-dice roll** — if you have 0 dots in a skill, roll **two** dice and take the **lowest**.
- **Push yourself / Assist** — spend 2 Stress for +1 die (or a better Effect) / spend 1 Stress to give a teammate +1 die.
- **Group action** — everyone rolls; the **best** die counts for the crew; the leader takes 1 Stress per teammate who rolled 1–3.
- **Harm** — lingering injury in four levels: 1 hampers you, 2 costs a die, 3 means you need help, 4 means you're out.
- **Load** — how much gear you carry on a job (Light 3, Normal 5, Heavy 6 items); you decide *which* items during the job.
- **Overindulge** — clearing more Stress than you had; your character goes overboard and something happens.
- **Physicker** — a back-alley medic who heals your Harm in Downtime.
- **Desperate = XP** — every Desperate action roll earns 1 XP in that skill's attribute.

## The world
- **The Ministry** — the government body that keeps the supernatural secret. They send your jobs in damp envelopes.
- **Apex Aegis** — a rich private firm that treats the supernatural like a mine.
- **The cover-up** — keeping the public from knowing monsters are real. If it fails, you lose your license and maybe more.
- **Resonance** — the faint signature that supernatural things leave behind. Ministry and Apex scanners look for it.
- **Anchor Item** — a personal object tied to a hidden creature. Take it and their disguise starts to fail. Every one belongs to somebody.
- **Twice-Born** — people who live in both the human world and something older.
- **Biological / Relic** — loot: perishable monster parts / cursed, active objects.
- **Containment Suite** — the crew's hidden lab and vault behind the bookshop's Zoltar machine.
- **Cursed Family Heirloom** — an object your character should have left behind. The GM will quietly do something with it.
- **Anchor Ledger** — the crew's record of every Anchor decision they've made.
