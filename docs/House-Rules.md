# House Rules

We play *Blades in the Dark* with a few table agreements. If you know the game, this is the whole list.

## Changes to the rules
- **Starting crew:** weak hold and 0 Coin (the book starts you strong with 2 Coin). You build up from nothing.
- **Heat and Wanted:** exactly as the book. Heat is the track you will see on the TV. At **Heat 9** the crew gains **+1 Wanted level** and Heat clears. Wanted level makes the consequences after a score more serious.
- **Entanglement:** after every score, as the book says. Heat and Wanted decide how bad. They are usually a cost of doing business, not a punishment.
- **Resistance:** after a consequence, the Co-GM will ask "You can resist that: want to?" through Session 3; after that, call for it yourself. Commit to the resistance before you roll it.
- **Devil's Bargains:** anyone at the table can offer one.

## Who rolls
You roll every action and resistance roll. The GM never rolls against you. The GM's only dice are the ones the book gives them: the Entanglement and fortune rolls for NPCs and clocks.

## How we run a night
- The contract card goes on the TV within ten minutes of starting. Planning gets about eight minutes before we go with whatever is on top.
- Every PC should roll in the key scene; if you have been quiet, expect the spotlight.
- One big reveal per night. A second one waits for next week.
- A rules question gets a minute. If it can't be settled, the GM rules and we check the book afterwards.
- We stop at the agreed time. A late score gets "cliffhanger here, or a five-minute fast-forward?"
- **Short night (90 minutes):** engagement roll, one score, downtime as one sentence per person.
- **If you miss a night:** your character is on the job but offscreen. You still earn the crew's XP and half of your own triggers. Others may only Assist for you.

## End of night
XP, then three quick questions: one highlight, one thing you want *more* of, one thing you want *less* of. Your answers shape what we play next.

## If a character dies or a player leaves
A fallen or retired character's Heirloom goes to the lair's vault. A new character joins through the story (a hunter from the lair's past, or someone asking for work), and the crew's Friends and Rivals carry over. If a player leaves, their character retires on screen or becomes a contact.
