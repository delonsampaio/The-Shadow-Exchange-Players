## 2. A few things on the sheet to ignore (this isn't that city)

The official sheets are written for the game's default setting. **Our campaign is in a different city**, so:

- **Heritage list** (Akoros, Skovlan, etc.): ignore it. **Write your own heritage** — wherever *your* character is from.
- **"Dangerous Friends" names** (the list of five NPCs): ignore the *names*. Use the **type** as a starting point ("a pugilist," "a physicker," "an extortionist") and **invent a name** that belongs in our world. The GM will help at Session 0.
- **"Duskwall" or setting names** you might see elsewhere on the site: not ours.
- Everything else on the sheet — **special abilities, action dots, Vice, backgrounds, Load, XP** — works exactly as written.

*(Backgrounds on the sheet: Academic, Labor, Law, Trade, Military, Noble, Underworld.)*
