## The 3 steps

- [ ] **Read the [Quick Start](Quick-Start.md)** — nothing else is required reading.
- [ ] **Build your character** with the [Character Creation Guide](Character-Creation-Guide.md) (it walks you through it).
- [ ] **Send the GM these 8 things** — a text, a message, or an email is fine; the GM will tell you where to send it:
    1. Your **playbook**
    2. **Name** (and alias), **look** (three details), **heritage and background** (one specific line), and your **Vice + Purveyor**
    3. Your **action dots**
    4. Your **starting special ability**
    5. Your **Close Friend** — name + one sentence
    6. Your **Rival** — name + one sentence
    7. Your **Cursed Family Heirloom** — just the object
    8. Your answer to **one question** (the Guide gives you three to choose from)


## Need your playbook sheet?
**Free PDFs for all seven playbooks: [Official Resources](Official-Resources.md).** *(Ignore the sheet's heritage list and friend names — they're for a different city.)* **No sheet yet? Come anyway** — we'll pick your special ability and your Friend/Rival at Session 0 in two minutes.

## Not stuck? Skip ahead
- **Not sure about your dots?** Take the playbook's suggestions. We'll fix it at the table.
- **No Friend or Rival yet?** Come anyway. We'll invent them in Session 0.
- **Don't have an idea for the Heirloom?** A watch, a photograph, a coat, a key. Pick the first thing that comes to mind.

## What happens at Session 0
Read [Session 0 Agenda](Session-0-Agenda.md) if you'd like to know. The short version: we introduce our characters, we look at the bookshop, we do **one silly practice roll**, and nothing bad happens to anyone.

*Bring: your sheet (or photo), your sense of humor, and your character's worst idea.*
