# Known Contacts


People the crew has found, built trust with, and returns to. Not friends, exactly — relationships that work because both sides understand what the other needs.

*This page will grow as the campaign does.*

---

### Harlan Okafor

Ex-Ministry field operative, now running a diner forty minutes outside the city. He left the Ministry under circumstances he hasn't explained and hasn't been asked to explain. He knows the organizational structure, the personnel rotations, the Ministry's internal politics, and — more usefully — what gets quietly buried and why.

He charges fair prices: information for information, or coin when information isn't available. He has never been wrong about a Ministry matter.

*Find him at his diner. He's there most days. He prefers you call ahead.*


---

### Greta Fenn

Ministry Field Compliance Officer. You have mailed her your incident forms for two years; she has returned each one annotated, with a form number, and no signature but hers. You have never met her. She has opinions about your handwriting.

*Her office is at the Ministry's Old City field office, six blocks east. Plain door. No sign.*
