# FAQ

**Do I need to know Blades in the Dark?**
No. Read the [Quick Start](Quick-Start.md). The GM and the Co-GM explain each mechanic the first time it comes up.

**I'm used to D&D. What's the biggest difference?**
There's no hit points, no initiative, and the GM never rolls. Most rolls give you *both* what you wanted and a cost. You can **flash back** to planning instead of planning in advance.

**Where do I get my playbook sheet?**
Free PDFs for all seven playbooks are on [Official Resources](Official-Resources.md). If you can't open one tonight, no problem — choose your playbook, Vice and Heirloom, and we'll pick your special ability and Friend/Rival at Session 0 in about two minutes.

**How do we do a heist without planning it first?**
We pick a quick approach ("we walk in as health inspectors") and make one *engagement roll* that drops us already inside. When you hit a snag, you can spend 0–2 Stress to **flash back**: "Actually, I bribed the night guard yesterday."

**How does an AI Co-GM work at the table?**
The human GM runs the game and makes the calls. The AI Co-GM helps describe places, voices characters, and updates the shared TV with scene images and in-world documents. **Anything on the TV is something your character can see.**

**Should we coordinate our three characters first?**
No need. Any mix works — though one talker/planner (Slide or Spider) or tinkerer (Leech) alongside a sneak or fighter makes things smoother. If you all pick the same playbook, the GM will say so.

**What if I can't think of what my character would say?**
Short answers are fine. The GM will build around whatever you give. You'll also get a menu of options when you're stuck. There is no wrong answer.

**What if I roll badly?**
It's usually *more interesting*, not worse. You can resist most consequences. Bad rolls are where the best stories come from.

**Can my character die?**
They can, but it's slow and you'll see it coming. Harm builds up, and most scenes are about the cover-up, not violence.

**What's a "Cursed Family Heirloom"?**
One object your character carries that they should have left behind. You only choose the object. The GM does the rest.

**How funny is this campaign?**
Quite. Expect a cat who is always asleep on the evidence, a lot of paperwork, and at least one creature with strong opinions. The quiet moments are quiet.

**Is there a lot of homework?**
No. Three things: [Quick Start](Quick-Start.md), build your character, send the GM your details. Everything else is optional reference.

**What if I can't make a session?**
Tell the GM. We'll work around you — your character can be "lost in their Vice" off-camera.
